The term

One session, one technical focus

Each row is a session: its main technical focus, and how much of its checklist you have ticked. Sessions that have not happened yet are dimmed.

    How to use this

    Your ticks live in this browser only

    Ticking a skill

    Check a skill only when you can do the “You can…” sentence without notes — not when you have seen the slide. A half-remembered skill left unchecked is more useful to you than a tidy page. Each session page is printable, so you can carry the focus, the checklist and the interview questions into a lab.

    Privacy

    Your ticks are stored in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere, no account is involved, and the instructor cannot see them. Clearing your browser data clears your progress.

How to use this

Check a skill only when you can do the “You can…” sentence without notes — not when you have seen the slide. A half-remembered skill left unchecked is more useful to you than a tidy page. Work session by session; each session page is printable, so you can carry the checklist into a lab.

Privacy: your ticks are stored in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere, no account is involved, and the instructor cannot see them. Clearing your browser data clears your progress.
